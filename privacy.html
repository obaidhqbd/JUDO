TRANSPARENCY & CONTROL

Privacy Policy.

This notice explains how the portfolio website uses analytics and how visitors can control that choice.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

In brief: this is a personal portfolio hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses Google Analytics 4 to understand overall site traffic, but the current implementation keeps Analytics disabled until you select “Accept analytics.” You can reject or change your choice at any time using “Privacy choices” at the bottom of the portfolio.

This page describes the current site setup and is general information, not legal advice.

1. Who operates this website?

This portfolio is operated by Mohammed Obaidul Hoque and published at obaidhqbd.github.io/JUDO. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information required to deliver and secure the site under its own Privacy Statement.

2. Google Analytics

If you accept analytics, this website loads Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID G-R8YP3TWCJN) to understand how people use the portfolio. Depending on the browser and the Analytics settings, information may include:

  • Pages viewed, page titles, approximate visit times, and interactions with the website.
  • Referring website or traffic source, when available.
  • Browser, operating system, device category, and general geographic area.
  • Cookies or similar identifiers used to distinguish visits and help measure site usage.

The purpose is to measure traffic and improve the usefulness and performance of the portfolio. I do not intentionally ask Analytics visitors to submit names, email addresses, passwords, or other directly identifying details through this site. Please do not place sensitive or personal information in page URLs or messages to third-party services.

Google processes Analytics data under its applicable terms and policies. Read Google's information about safeguarding Analytics data and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Cookies and your choice

Analytics is not loaded until you choose “Accept analytics.” If you select “Reject analytics,” this site stores that preference in your browser and does not load its Google Analytics tag. The preference is stored locally in your browser; it is not an account or login.

You can change your decision by selecting Privacy choices in the portfolio footer and choosing either option. Clearing browser site data may also clear your saved choice. You can additionally manage cookies in your browser or use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Choosing to reject Analytics does not prevent GitHub or other linked services from processing information as part of their own operation. External websites have their own privacy policies.

4. Advertising and third-party links

This portfolio currently does not intentionally display third-party ads or load advertising/remarketing tags. It contains links to services such as GitHub and social networks. If you follow those links, those services may collect information under their own terms. This policy should be updated if additional advertising or tracking services are added later.

5. Data access, retention, and security

Analytics reports are available to the portfolio owner through the Google Analytics property. Data retention and deletion settings are managed in that property and by the service provider. No website can promise absolute security, but access to the Analytics account should be protected with a strong password and available account-security features.

6. Children's privacy

This portfolio is a professional information website. It does not provide user accounts or an on-site form for submitting personal details, and it is not designed to solicit personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

This notice may be updated when the site's features, analytics setup, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.

8. Contact

For questions about this website's privacy practices, contact the site operator through the GitHub profile or LinkedIn profile.

Because this is a small personal portfolio, this notice is intended to explain the current implementation in plain language. It is not a guarantee of compliance with every country's privacy laws.
